01 / Our beginning

Less paperwork.
More possibility.

It started with a simple question: why should opening a bank account mean a stack of forms and a trip across town?

Andrew Pringle founded CAM Credit Union LLC with a vision for a better banking experience. Alongside Cofounder and Bookkeeper Mat Barret and CEO Carter McDougal, he brings together a passion for business, a love of problem-solving, and a belief that people deserve clear, friendly service.

Our business offers checking and savings accounts, loans, and credit cards. Our next chapter is moving beyond paper forms toward digital applications and better-organized records, with a personal connection at the heart of it all.
